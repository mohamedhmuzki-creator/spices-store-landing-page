Our story — since 1987

A family spice businessin Mawathagama, Kurunegala.

M.R Traders was started in 1987 by M. R. M. Musthakeen at 96, Kandy Road, Mawathagama in the Kurunegala District — buying spices and produce from farmers across the North Western Province and selling them in bulk.

How we work

Our process, in four steps.

The same routine we have followed since 1987 — from the farm gate to your godown.

Step 01

We buy from local farms

Farmers bring cinnamon, pepper, copra and more to our counter in Mawathagama — or we collect from the farm gate.

Step 02

We grade & weigh fairly

Every lot is cleaned, graded by hand and weighed in front of you at an agreed daily rate.

Step 03

We pack your bulk order

Sacks, bales or crates packed to order — from small trade loads to full truck orders.

Step 04

We deliver across the island

Loaded at Mawathagama and delivered anywhere in Sri Lanka. Today's rate is one WhatsApp message away.

No middlemen, no waiting.
